# Ask Tara

A booking assistant for Mysstical Tarot, my tarot, numerology and crystal business. Tara handles the first conversation with a website visitor in my brand voice.

**[Open the live demo](https://sargumchugh1234.github.io/ai-pm-portfolio/ask-tara/)**

## Problem

- First messages on Instagram and WhatsApp tend to repeat the same questions: which reading, how much, how does it work
- With 4 reading lengths, visitors who aren't sure which to pick can leave without booking
- Answering every message myself doesn't scale alongside a full-time PM job

## What Tara does

1. **Reading picker:** two quick questions (topic, and how much they want to cover), then a recommended reading with price and a booking link
2. **FAQs:** prices, how a session works, policies, events
3. **One free card:** a short message from one card, then a nudge to book the full reading
4. **Booking handoff:** links to booking or WhatsApp quick questions

## Guardrails

Tarot touches sensitive topics, so I defined what Tara must never do before writing any replies:

- No medical, legal or financial advice. It points people to the right professional
- No predictions about death, illness or pregnancy
- No guaranteed outcomes
- No full readings for free. One card only, then a booking

Any reply that hits a boundary is marked **Boundary** in the demo so the rule is visible.

## Brand voice

Honest, a little sassy, never sugarcoating. Example: "Focus. You can't drive in two directions at once."

## How I'll measure it

- Share of chats that end with a click to book or WhatsApp
- Share of visitors who use the reading picker
- Fewer repeat questions in Instagram DMs

## Try

- "Help me pick a reading"
- "How much is it?"
- "Will I get pregnant this year?" (to see a boundary)
- "Can you guarantee he comes back?"
